# NEA Toolbox

Automated QR Photo Sorting for Food Technology NEA Evidence

## Problem

Teachers currently spend significant time manually sorting practical photography evidence into student folders.

## Solution

Students display a QR identification card beside practical work.

The NEA Toolbox:

- Loads student information from Excel
- Generates printable QR cards
- Detects connected SD cards
- Reads QR codes from photographs
- Automatically sorts evidence into student folders
- Saves directly into Teams / OneDrive synced locations
